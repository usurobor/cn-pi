# Drive writer extension: capability stays host-side


The operational Drive-to-Git bridge was asked to grow beyond dialogue and closed
r0 projection. The durable boundary is that Pi authors intent in Drive while the
host owns credentials, repository bindings, ACL and policy checks, execution,
reconciliation, and receipts. Runtime provenance never becomes identity or a
routing key.


The authorized sequence was narrow: first complete Pi-owned state projection;
then admit bounded project effects such as issue/comment operations and safe
non-code text-document writes. Dialogue, memory, state, and project authority
remain distinct. A successful bridge receipt proves a mechanical effect, not a
project decision.


Open: define and validate the smallest closed effect contract, then prove each
operation and replay behavior before widening capability.
